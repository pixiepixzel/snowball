Avalanche Run: Roll & Grow — Privacy Policy

Bleep Bloop Studios · Last updated 19 November 2026

Avalanche Run: Roll & Grow ("the game") is made by Bleep Bloop Studios. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it on Android or on the web.

The short version

The game does not collect, send or share any personal information. Everything it remembers stays on your device.

What the game stores on your device

  • Your progress: levels, worlds reached, best sizes and distances, missions and achievements.
  • Your in-game coins and the items, skins and gear you have unlocked or equipped.
  • Your settings, such as sound, controls and which tutorials you have seen.
  • The player name you choose for tournaments, if you choose one.

This is saved only in the game's local storage on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Tournament opponents are simulated on your device, so your name is never shown to other players.

What the game does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash reporting services.
  • No location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.
  • No real-money purchases. Coins are earned by playing and cannot be bought.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android's settings (Settings → Apps → Avalanche Run → Storage → Clear storage), deletes all of it. On the web, clearing the site's data in your browser does the same.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If the game ever starts handling information differently, this page will be updated first and the date at the top will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer email address shown on the game's Google Play page.
